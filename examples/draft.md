# A draft about a colleague

He joined the team in spring, and he brought two notebooks and a habit of asking why. His first
review found a bug nobody else had seen, and he wrote it up the same afternoon.

The manager said "he is the most careful reviewer we have" in the meeting, and that is a quote, so
his words should stay his words.

Run `he --help` before the first commit; the tool prints his own usage there.

He types quickly and he runs the tests twice, which is how he catches the flaky ones.

Dana asked whether he was coming to the standup, and nobody had told him yet.
